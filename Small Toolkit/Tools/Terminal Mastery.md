#### Panes

SPLIT new Pane LEFT/RIGHT
``Ctrl E``

SPLIT new Pane UP/DOWN
``Ctrl O``

CLOSE current Pane
`Ctrl Shift W`

NAVIGATE Panes
``Alt ArrowKeys``

ZOOM current Pane
`Ctrl Mouse Scroll`

#### Tabs

OPEN new Tab
``Ctrl Shift T``

CLOSE ALL
`Ctrl Shift Q`
