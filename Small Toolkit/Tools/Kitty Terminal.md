#### Panes

SPLIT new Pane LEFT/RIGHT
``Ctrl \``

SPLIT new Pane UP/DOWN
``Ctrl =``

CLOSE current Pane
`Ctrl Shift W`

REARRANGE current Pane Layout
`Ctrl Shift L`

ZOOM current Pane
`Ctrl Shift Z`

#### Tabs

OPEN new Tab
``Ctrl Shift T``

CLOSE new Tab
`Ctrl Shift Q`

#### Typing

BEGINNING of Line
``Ctrl A``

END of Line
`Ctrl E`

REVERSE search history
``Ctrl R ``

CLEAR screen
``Ctrl L``
